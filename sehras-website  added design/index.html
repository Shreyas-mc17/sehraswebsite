Beyond the ceremony

Signature experiences
for your guests.

Live Photo Station

Instant memories, printed on the spot.

A fully styled live photo station set up right at your venue — guests step in, strike a pose, and walk away with a printed keepsake within seconds. Digital copies are shared instantly too, so every memory travels home with your guests the same night.

Celebrity Makeup Artist

Red-carpet artistry, for your big day.

We bring in celebrity-trained makeup artists to work with the bride, family, and guests — from bridal looks to touch-up stations through the evening — so everyone in every photograph looks and feels their absolute best.

Live Sketch Artist

A portrait, drawn while you celebrate.

A live sketch artist sets up at the celebration and captures guests in quick, characterful portraits — a personal, one-of-a-kind favour that becomes a keepsake nobody else's wedding thought to offer.
